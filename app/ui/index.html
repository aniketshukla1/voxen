              Privacy & data

              Everything Voxen keeps stays on this Mac, in plain files you can read and edit.

              On this Mac

              • Your keysReadable only by you
                .env
              • Your bots and roomsEdit it by hand any time
                team.toml
              • MemoryWhat every bot knows about you
                data/memory.md
              • TasksReminders and repeating tasks
                data/tasks.json
              • PicturesThe pictures your bots make
                data/pictures/

              What leaves this Mac

              • Deepgram hears what you sayOnly while voice is on.
              • The bot's AI provider answersIt runs the web searches too. X search and pictures go through xAI.
              • ElevenLabs speaks the answersWhile voice is on, and when a task's answer is said out loud.
              • Chats aren't savedThey're gone when Voxen quits. Memory and tasks stay until you forget or cancel them.
