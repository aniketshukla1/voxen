    Set up Voxen

    Voxen runs on your own keys: three it needs, and xAI if you want Grok's tools too. They stay on this Mac, in the project's .env file, readable only by you.

    ClaudeMissing

    Thinks and writes every line. Needed for anything. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.

    DeepgramMissing

    Listens when you talk. Needed for voice. Get a key at console.deepgram.com.

    ElevenLabsMissing

    Gives every voice its sound. Needed for voice. Get a key at elevenlabs.io under Developers.

    xAIOptional

    Adds Grok's own tools: searching X, and making pictures with Grok Imagine. Get a key at console.x.ai.
