    Bots

    Each bot has its own voice, personality and model. The main bot is the one you talk to when Voxen opens.

      Rooms

      Group chats where two or more bots take turns and talk it over with you.

        Voice

        Talk to your bots and hear each one answer in its own voice.

        Tips

        • Use headphones. Through speakers the bots hear themselves and stop.
        • Talk over a bot, press Esc or click Stop to cut it off.
        • ⌘D turns voice on and off from anywhere.
        • Each bot's voice and speaking speed are in Bots, under Edit.
