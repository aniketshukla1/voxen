# Voxen brand guidelines v1.2

## Quick reference

- **Name:** Voxen. Written "Voxen" in sentences; the wordmark's form comes with the new look.
- **Meaning:** *vox* is Latin for voice, and *-en* is the old English plural, as in oxen.
  Voxen means "voices": a team of them, talking with you and with each other.
- **Say it:** VOX-en.
- **Tagline:** Your team of voices.
- **Promise:** Talk to one of your bots or to all of them. They listen, talk it through, and
  get things done.

## Look: the stage (approved 2026-11-04)

- **Concept:** Voxen is a stage, not a chat app. A room's voices sit at the top as its cast;
  the conversation runs below as a script.
- **Voiceprints:** each voice's mark is a ring of sound bars shaped by its name, so no two
  match. It rests, breathes while listening, sweeps while thinking and moves while speaking.
  The Voxen logo is the voiceprint of the word "voxen" in white. The app icon is that print
  with finer bars, on a black squircle (made by a script that copies the app's drawing code).
- **Colour:** black and white. Each voice brings one hue (Iris `#8E7CFF`, Tide `#2EC4B6`,
  Ember `#FF6B3D`, Sol `#FFC233`, Sky `#5AB0FF`, Bloom `#FF5FA2`); nothing else is coloured.
- **Type:** chat text is plain and quiet, like Claude's apps: SF Pro at 13px, regular weight,
  every line the same size; the newest one fades in word by word. New York (serif) is kept for
  the big moments: the empty-room welcome line and dialog titles. You and the interface use SF Pro. Names and labels are small capitals with wide spacing.
- **Shape:** buttons, cards, inputs and dialogs have 8px corners (`--radius` in the app's
  style.css). Only voices are round: voiceprints, colour swatches and status dots.
- **Motion:** a spark carries the turn from one voice to the next in group rooms. All motion
  stops with the Mac's Reduce Motion setting.
- **Retired:** the first look (violet brand colour, glossy orbs, rounded type).

## Voice and tone

**Personality:** warm, quick, clear, a little playful. A good friend who happens to know
things, never a corporate assistant.

| We are | We aren't |
|---|---|
| Warm | Gushing |
| Clear | Terse |
| Confident | Bossy |
| Playful | Silly |

**Tone by context:**
- Empty states invite: "Chat with Sam", "Ask Maya and Sam together".
- Buttons start with a verb: "Create bot", "Start group", "Save and restart".
- Errors say what happened, then what to do: "Voice needs your ElevenLabs key. Add it in Setup."
- Bots are people with first names. The product never calls them "AI assistants".

**Avoid:** "AI-powered", "seamless", "leverage", "simply", "just", "please", exclamation marks
in system copy.
